# Best Hairstyles for Oblong Face Shape

If you have an oblong face shape, the best hairstyles usually reduce visual length and add more width.

Oblong faces are longer than they are wide, so the wrong hairstyle can make the face look even longer. The right cut adds balance by shortening the visual vertical line and creating more side presence.

## What Defines an Oblong Face Shape?

You likely have an oblong face if:

- Your face is clearly longer than it is wide
- Your forehead, cheekbones, and jawline are fairly similar in width
- Your facial outline looks straighter than round or heart shapes

If you are unsure whether you are oval or oblong, start with [face shape types explained](/blog/face-shape-types-explained) or use [what face shape do I have](/what-face-shape-do-i-have).

## Best Hairstyles for Oblong Face Shape

## 1. Shoulder-Length Cuts

Shoulder-length cuts are one of the safest options for oblong faces.

### Why it works

- Add width without extra height
- Keep the face from looking too stretched
- Work well with waves and layers

## 2. Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs can help reduce visible forehead length.

### Why it works

- Shorten the upper face visually
- Add width around the eyes and cheeks
- Pair well with medium and long styles

## 3. Waves and Curls

Texture is usually your friend if you have an oblong face.

### Why it works

- Adds width at the sides
- Breaks up long straight vertical lines
- Makes proportions feel more balanced

## 4. Layered Lob

A textured long bob can work very well.

### Why it works

- Adds side volume
- Avoids dragging the face downward
- Keeps the cut modern and easy to manage

## 5. Fringe Styles

Soft bangs or fringe can often improve balance dramatically.

### Why it works

- Reduce the effect of face length
- Create stronger horizontal focus
- Make the face feel more proportionate

## Best Men's Hairstyles for Oblong Face Shape

These styles often work well for men:

- Textured crop
- Side-swept fringe
- Medium-length layered cut
- Styles with moderate top volume only

### Why these work

- Avoid too much extra height
- Add width or texture
- Keep the face from looking too narrow and long

## Hairstyles to Be Careful With

These often exaggerate oblong shape:

- Very long straight hair with no width
- High pompadours
- Slick backs with strong vertical lift
- Cuts with super-tight sides and lots of height on top

If your face is already long, adding more height is usually a bad trade.

## Styling Tips for Oblong Faces

- Add width, not height
- Use texture to break long lines
- Consider bangs to shorten the face visually
- Avoid excessive crown volume
- Keep side fullness balanced and intentional

## Quick Cheat Sheet

### Best choices

- Shoulder-length cuts
- Curtain bangs
- Waves and curls
- Layered lob
- Textured crop with fringe

### Watch out for

- Very long flat hair
- High pompadours
- Tight sides with tall top volume

## Final Takeaway

The best hairstyles for oblong face shape reduce visual length and add side balance. Width, texture, and strategic fringe usually work better than more height.
