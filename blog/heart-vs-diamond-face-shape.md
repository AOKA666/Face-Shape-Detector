# Heart vs Diamond Face Shape: What’s the Difference?

Heart and diamond face shapes get mixed up all the time.

That makes sense. Both can have narrower chins, visible cheekbones, and a more tapered lower face. But they are not identical, and the difference matters when you are choosing bangs, glasses, contour placement, or hairstyle width.

## The Short Answer

The simplest difference is this:

- **Heart face shape** = forehead is wider than the cheekbones and jawline
- **Diamond face shape** = cheekbones are the widest point, while forehead and jawline are narrower

That is the main distinction.

## What a Heart Face Shape Looks Like

A heart face usually has:

- Wider forehead
- Noticeable taper toward the chin
- Narrow or pointed chin
- Upper face that feels broader than the lower face

The overall silhouette feels top-heavy compared with the jawline.

## What a Diamond Face Shape Looks Like

A diamond face usually has:

- Cheekbones as the widest point
- Narrower forehead
n- Narrower jawline
- More angular facial structure
- Chin that may look pointed but not always strongly tapered from the forehead

The overall shape feels widest in the middle of the face.

## 4 Ways to Tell the Difference

## 1. Check the Forehead Width

### Heart

- Forehead is usually the widest visible point

### Diamond

- Forehead is narrower than the cheekbones

If the top of your face clearly dominates the shape, heart is more likely.

## 2. Check the Cheekbones

### Heart

- Cheekbones can be prominent, but they are not always the widest area

### Diamond

- Cheekbones are usually the widest point of the face

This is the strongest clue for diamond face shape.

## 3. Look at the Shape From Top to Bottom

### Heart

- Face tapers from wide forehead down to narrower chin

### Diamond

- Face widens at the cheekbones and narrows both upward and downward

Heart feels like a top-down taper. Diamond feels like a center-out shape.

## 4. Notice the Overall Sharpness

### Heart

- Can look softer depending on forehead and hairline

### Diamond

- Often looks more angular because the cheekbones stand out more strongly

## Styling Differences

## Hairstyles for Heart Face Shape

Heart-shaped faces often benefit from:

- Chin-level balance
- Side-swept bangs
- Curtain bangs
- Soft waves
- Styles that reduce upper-face emphasis

## Hairstyles for Diamond Face Shape

Diamond-shaped faces often benefit from:

- Styles that add width at the forehead or jaw area
- Side parts
- Layering that softens the cheekbone area
- Chin-length or shoulder-length balance

## How to Confirm It

Do this in a straight-on photo:

1. Pull your hair away from the face
2. Compare your forehead width with cheekbone width
3. See whether the widest point is at the top or in the middle

## Final Takeaway

Heart vs diamond face shape comes down to **where your face is widest**.

If the forehead is widest and the face narrows steadily toward the chin, heart is more likely. If the cheekbones are the widest point while the forehead and jawline stay narrower, diamond is more likely.
