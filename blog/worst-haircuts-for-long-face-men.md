# Worst Haircuts for Long Face Men: Styles That Make It Worse

If you have a long face, the wrong haircut can make it look even longer in about five seconds.

That is why this face shape needs more discipline than trend worship.

The worst haircuts for long face men usually do one of two things:

- add too much height on top
- remove too much width from the sides

Do both at once and congratulations, your haircut is now actively snitching on your proportions.

## What Counts as a Long Face?

A long face usually means your face is noticeably longer than it is wide. In face shape terms, that often overlaps with **oblong**.

Typical traits:

- longer vertical proportions
- similar width through forehead, cheekbones, and jaw
- a face that gets exaggerated by height-heavy cuts

## Worst Haircuts for Long Face Men

## 1. Tall Pompadour

This is one of the biggest offenders.

### Why it fails

- adds major vertical height
- makes the whole face look longer
- often comes with sides that are too tight

On a long face, this cut can look like a bad math problem.

## 2. High Skin Fade With Volume on Top

This one is everywhere, and it is often terrible for long faces.

### Why it fails

- removes side width aggressively
- increases contrast between top and sides
- pushes the eye upward, not outward

This is a great way to make an oblong face look even more stretched.

## 3. Slick Back With Lift

A slick back is not always bad, but the lifted version usually is.

### Why it fails

- exposes more forehead
- adds extra crown height
- emphasizes length from hairline to chin

## 4. Faux Hawk or Mohawk Variations

If the cut is basically shouting verticality, it is probably not helping a long face.

### Why it fails

- all the emphasis goes upward
- the sides are usually too tight
- the overall shape becomes narrow and tall

## 5. Extra-Long Flat Hair

Long hair can work on a long face, but long **flat** hair is another story.

### Why it fails

- drags the eye downward
- adds more visible vertical length
- creates a narrow silhouette if there is no side movement

## 6. Disconnected Undercut

The disconnected undercut often overdoes contrast.

### Why it fails

- isolates the top section
- keeps the sides too lean
- creates a long narrow head shape visually

## 7. Ultra-Tight Buzz With Nothing Else Going On

A buzz cut can work for some long faces, but the tightest version is risky.

### Why it fails

- exposes all face length directly
- removes opportunities for texture or balancing width
- can make the whole face feel harsher and longer

## Better Haircut Patterns for Long Face Men

Instead of chasing those mistakes, usually look for:

- textured fringe
- crew cut
- French crop
- side part with moderate volume
- taper cuts with more side presence
- medium-length cuts with movement

### Why these work

- reduce face length visually
- keep more width through the silhouette
- avoid overloading the crown with height

## Quick Rule: What Should Long Face Men Avoid?

Usually avoid cuts that are:

- too tall
- too tight at the sides
- too flat and long
- too narrow overall

That is the real pattern. Everything else is just variation.

## Final Takeaway

The worst haircuts for long face men are the ones that exaggerate what is already there. If your face is long, stop choosing cuts that scream "more length."

You want the opposite: less height, more balance, better width.
