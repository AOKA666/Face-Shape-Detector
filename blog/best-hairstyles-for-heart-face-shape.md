# Best Hairstyles for Heart Face Shape

If you have a heart face shape, the best hairstyles usually create balance between a wider forehead and a narrower chin.

Heart-shaped faces often look striking because the upper face is more prominent. The goal with hairstyles is usually to soften that top-heavy effect and add a little more visual balance around the lower face.

## What Defines a Heart Face Shape?

You likely have a heart face if:

- Your forehead is wider than your jawline
- Your chin is narrower or pointed
- Your face tapers downward
- Your cheekbones may also stand out

If you are not fully sure, compare it with [heart vs diamond face shape](/blog/heart-vs-diamond-face-shape) or use [what face shape do I have](/what-face-shape-do-i-have).

## Best Hairstyles for Heart Face Shape

## 1. Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs are one of the easiest wins for heart-shaped faces.

### Why it works

- Softens forehead width
- Frames the face naturally
- Creates balance without hiding features

## 2. Chin-Length Bob

A chin-length bob can add helpful visual weight to the lower face.

### Why it works

- Balances a narrower chin
- Adds structure around the jawline
- Keeps the face from feeling too top-heavy

## 3. Long Layers With Soft Waves

Long soft layers work especially well when the volume sits below the cheekbones.

### Why it works

- Adds width lower down
- Softens facial contrast
- Keeps the overall shape fluid

## 4. Side-Swept Bangs

A side fringe can reduce the impression of forehead width.

### Why it works

- Breaks the width of the upper face
- Adds asymmetry
- Keeps the look soft and flattering

## 5. Shoulder-Length Cuts With Movement

Medium-length styles often suit heart faces very well.

### Why it works

- Prevents all the visual weight from staying at the top
- Adds width closer to the jawline
- Gives flexible styling options

## Best Men's Hairstyles for Heart Face Shape

If you are choosing men's styles, these often work well:

- Side-swept textured top
- Medium-length textured cuts
- Fringe styles
- Tapered cuts with balanced top volume

### Why these work

- Avoid over-emphasizing the forehead
- Keep proportions more balanced
- Add softness around strong upper-face structure

## Hairstyles to Be Careful With

These can over-emphasize the upper face:

- Very high quiffs
- Extreme top volume with very tight sides
- Blunt full bangs cut too high
- Super-short cuts that expose all forehead width without balance

The issue is not that these are forbidden. The issue is proportion.

## Styling Tips for Heart Face Shape

- Add softness around the forehead
- Add some width near the jaw or lower face
- Use movement instead of rigid shape
- Keep top volume controlled
- Use bangs strategically if forehead width feels dominant

## Quick Cheat Sheet

### Best choices

- Curtain bangs
- Chin-length bob
- Long soft layers
- Side-swept bangs
- Shoulder-length cuts

### Watch out for

- Too much volume on top
- Very tight sides with high height
- Cuts that exaggerate forehead width

## Final Takeaway

The best hairstyles for heart face shape help balance a broader upper face with a narrower chin. Soft fringe, lower-face width, and controlled top volume usually work best.
