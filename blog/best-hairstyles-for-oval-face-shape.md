# Best Hairstyles for Oval Face Shape

If you have an oval face shape, congratulations: you have the most versatile face shape for hairstyles.

That does not mean every haircut is automatically a masterpiece. It means your proportions are balanced enough that you can pull off more styles than most people, as long as the cut still matches your hair texture and personal style.

## What Defines an Oval Face Shape?

You likely have an oval face if:

- Your face is slightly longer than it is wide
- Your forehead is a bit wider than your jawline
- Your jawline is soft and rounded
- Your features look balanced overall

Oval faces do not usually need heavy correction. That is why they can handle both structured and soft styles well.

## Best Hairstyles for Oval Face Shape

## 1. Long Layers

Long layers are one of the easiest and most flattering options for oval faces.

### Why it works

- Maintains natural balance
- Adds movement without distorting proportion
- Works with straight, wavy, and curly hair

This is a classic for a reason.

## 2. Blunt Bob

Oval faces can usually pull off a blunt bob better than most face shapes.

### Why it works

- Your proportions can handle cleaner lines
- The cut looks intentional rather than heavy
- It can feel modern, sharp, and polished

Just avoid going too long and too flat if your hair lacks volume.

## 3. Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs often look excellent on oval faces.

### Why it works

- Frames the eyes and cheekbones
- Adds softness and shape
- Works with medium and long lengths

If you want a change without committing to a dramatic cut, this is an easy move.

## 4. Shoulder-Length Waves

Shoulder-length waves are almost unfairly easy on oval faces.

### Why it works

- Preserve balance
- Add texture and body
- Suit casual and polished styling alike

## 5. Pixie Cut

Yes, oval faces often carry a pixie cut very well.

### Why it works

- Balanced proportions prevent the cut from feeling too severe
- Highlights cheekbones and eyes
- Can look elegant, edgy, or playful depending on styling

This is one of the few face shapes that can often handle very short hair without much struggle.

## Best Men's Hairstyles for Oval Face Shape

Oval faces are equally flexible for men's cuts.

### Best options

- Side part
- Quiff
- Pompadour
- Textured crop
- Buzz cut
- Slick back
- Medium-length layered styles

### Why these work

- The face is proportionally balanced
- Most top-heavy or structured styles still look controlled
- Short and long options both work

## Hairstyles to Be Careful With

Oval faces have range, but there are still a few traps:

- Very long, flat hair with no shape can drag the face down
- Extremely high volume on top can make the face look too long
- Narrow cuts with no width can over-elongate the face

So yes, oval is flexible. No, it is not invincible.

## Styling Tips for Oval Faces

- Keep the natural proportion balanced
- Add texture if the cut feels too plain
- Avoid excessive height at the crown if your face already leans long
- Use bangs if you want to shorten the face visually
- Match the haircut to your hair density and styling tolerance

## Quick Cheat Sheet

### Best choices

- Long layers
- Blunt bob
- Curtain bangs
- Shoulder-length waves
- Pixie cut
- Men's quiff, crop, side part, or buzz cut

### Watch out for

- Super-flat extra-long hair
- Too much vertical volume
- Cuts that make the face look stretched

## Final Takeaway

The best hairstyles for oval face shape are the ones that keep your natural balance while adding the right amount of personality, texture, or structure. You have options. A lot of them.

If you are unsure whether your face is oval or oblong, that difference matters. An oblong face usually needs less vertical emphasis, so check before committing to a style built around extra height.
