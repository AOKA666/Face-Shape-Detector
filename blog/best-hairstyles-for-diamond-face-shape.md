# Best Hairstyles for Diamond Face Shape

If you have a diamond face shape, the best hairstyles usually soften prominent cheekbones and add balance to a narrower forehead and jawline.

Diamond-shaped faces can look extremely striking, but the wrong haircut can exaggerate the width in the middle of the face. The goal is usually to create a smoother top-to-bottom balance.

## What Defines a Diamond Face Shape?

You likely have a diamond face if:

- Your cheekbones are the widest part of your face
- Your forehead is narrower
- Your jawline is narrower
- Your face may look more angular overall

If you are unsure whether your face is heart or diamond, read [heart vs diamond face shape](/blog/heart-vs-diamond-face-shape) or check [what face shape do I have](/what-face-shape-do-i-have).

## Best Hairstyles for Diamond Face Shape

## 1. Side Part

A side part is one of the easiest ways to soften symmetry and redistribute visual width.

### Why it works

- Reduces cheekbone emphasis
- Adds shape at the top of the face
- Makes the look feel more balanced

## 2. Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs often flatter diamond faces well.

### Why it works

- Add width around the forehead area
- Soften angularity
- Pair well with medium and long cuts

## 3. Shoulder-Length Layers

Layered shoulder-length cuts are a strong option for diamond face shapes.

### Why it works

- Add softness around the face
- Prevent all the width from sitting at the cheekbones
- Keep the lower face from looking too narrow

## 4. Chin-Length Bob With Texture

A textured bob can work well when it creates balance instead of blunt width.

### Why it works

- Brings more presence to the jaw area
- Softens the contrast between cheekbones and chin
- Looks polished without feeling too severe

## 5. Soft Waves

Loose waves are usually a safe and flattering choice.

### Why it works

- Reduce angular sharpness
- Frame the cheekbones more softly
- Create a more blended silhouette

## Best Men's Hairstyles for Diamond Face Shape

For men, these cuts often work best:

- Textured fringe
- Side-parted medium cuts
- Slightly fuller side balance
- Medium-length layered styles

### Why these work

- Help balance forehead and jaw area
- Avoid over-exposing cheekbone width
- Add softness and structure together

## Hairstyles to Be Careful With

These can over-emphasize the middle of the face:

- Very tight sides with volume only on top
- Super-slicked-back styles with no softness
- Cuts that expose the full cheekbone structure without balance
- Severe center-part styles if they exaggerate facial narrowness

## Styling Tips for Diamond Faces

- Add width or softness near the forehead
- Add some structure near the jawline
- Avoid cuts that isolate cheekbone width
- Use layers to blend transitions
- Prefer movement over severe geometry

## Quick Cheat Sheet

### Best choices

- Side part
- Curtain bangs
- Shoulder-length layers
- Soft waves
- Textured bob

### Watch out for

- Extreme slick-back styles
- Tight sides with exposed cheekbone width
- Harsh cuts with no softness

## Final Takeaway

The best hairstyles for diamond face shape soften prominent cheekbones and create better balance around the forehead and jawline. Side parts, layers, and soft texture usually do the job well.
