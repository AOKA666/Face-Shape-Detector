# Best Hairstyles for Round Face Shape

If you have a round face shape, the best hairstyles are usually the ones that add height, structure, and a little vertical movement.

Round faces tend to have softer angles, fuller cheeks, and similar width and length. That means the wrong haircut can make the face look wider, while the right one can create a much more balanced look.

## What Defines a Round Face Shape?

You likely have a round face if:

- Your face width and length are close
- Your cheeks are the fullest part of the face
- Your jawline is soft rather than angular
- Your face has few sharp contours

The goal with hairstyles is not to hide that. The goal is to shape the overall silhouette more intentionally.

## Best Hairstyles for Round Face Shape

## 1. Long Layers

Long layers help elongate the face and reduce visual heaviness around the cheeks.

### Why it works

- Adds vertical movement
- Prevents the hair from sitting like one wide block
- Frames the face without making it look wider

This is one of the safest high-win options.

## 2. Side Part

A side part breaks facial symmetry and helps a round face appear longer and more structured.

### Why it works

- Creates asymmetry
- Adds lift at the roots
- Draws attention diagonally instead of outward

Center parts can work too, but side parts are usually more forgiving on round faces.

## 3. Lob With Texture

A textured long bob can work very well if it falls below the chin.

### Why it works

- Adds shape without cutting at the widest part of the face
- Gives movement instead of blunt width
- Keeps things modern and easy to style

The key is length. A bob that stops at the cheeks can backfire.

## 4. Long Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs can flatter a round face when they open the face instead of closing it off.

### Why it works

- Creates vertical framing
- Softens the cheek area
- Adds shape around the forehead without making the face look short

Avoid super-heavy straight-across bangs unless you specifically want a shorter face effect.

## 5. High Ponytail or Updo With Volume

Styles with height at the crown usually work well on round faces.

### Why it works

- Adds visual length
- Pulls attention upward
- Creates a cleaner face outline

Flat styles tend to emphasize width. Volume is your friend here.

## 6. Layered Shag With Height

A modern shag or wolf-inspired cut can work if it creates top volume and texture rather than side bulk.

### Why it works

- Adds edge and structure
- Breaks up softness
- Gives movement that offsets roundness

The trick is controlled chaos, not puffball energy.

## Best Men's Hairstyles for Round Face Shape

If you are looking for men's styles, these usually work best:

- Textured quiff
- Pompadour
- Side part with short sides
- Faux hawk
- Undercut with volume on top
- Messy top with tapered sides

### Why these work

- Keep the sides tighter
- Add height on top
- Create a longer face effect

## Hairstyles to Be Careful With

These are not banned, but they often make a round face look rounder:

- Chin-length blunt bobs
- Full heavy bangs
- Very short cuts with no top volume
- One-length cuts with width at the cheeks
- Round afros or curls with all the volume on the sides only

Again, the issue is not the haircut itself. It is where the volume sits.

## Styling Tips for Round Faces

- Add root lift at the crown
- Keep some length below the chin
- Use texture instead of width
- Avoid too much bulk at cheek level
- Use face-framing pieces strategically

## Quick Cheat Sheet

### Best choices

- Long layers
- Side part
- Textured lob
- Curtain bangs
- High-volume updos
- Men's quiff or pompadour

### Less flattering choices

- Cheek-length blunt cuts
- Flat center-part styles with no lift
- Full straight bangs
- Wide, bulky side-heavy styles

## Final Takeaway

The best hairstyles for a round face shape are the ones that create length and definition without adding extra width at the cheeks. Think height, texture, and movement.

If you are not sure whether your face is round or oval, use a face shape detector first. Better that than walking into a haircut with pure delusion.
